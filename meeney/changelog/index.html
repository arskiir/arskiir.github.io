Changelog

What each version of Meeney brought, newest first.

  1. 0.46.1

    October 25, 2026

    Fixed

    • The Spending map stays where you moved it when you edit an amount or a date, and its list and total no longer blink empty or show the wrong currency while it regroups after a new period, slice or currency. Plus
    • On the Spending map, a spot counting up during a replay keeps counting as you pinch across a zoom level, instead of jumping to its total. Plus
    • A replay ends cleanly when you pick a spot's tag as it finishes, or when Analytics switches to All in its currency. Plus
    • A place read from your phone, including Where I am now, is checked with a new fix when the phone's last point is old, so it lands where you are, and the place shown stays if no new fix comes.
    • After you save, Home scrolls to the new transaction and highlights it even when a type filter was on, or the transaction's place arrived while you were away.
    • A place shown on the form is kept when you save and lock your phone straight away.
    • Past midnight, a new transaction picked for yesterday no longer offers a currency from where you are, nor reads your location from Try again or Allow.
    • The amount calculator stops at two decimals for every key, so what you type is what is saved.
    • Monthly figures and the net balance chart show 0 instead of −0.
    • Spending pace's legend starts with a capital for a week or a custom period.
    • Backup, restore and Reset all data can't run on Demo data from a page left open as Demo mode turned on.
    • Settings › Categories no longer says categories were deleted when Demo mode turns on or off.
    • Every Demo mode switch shows "Making Demo data…" while it works, including one turned on from a search.
    • A setting opened from search is shown again when you tap the result again on a wide window, and not again when the window changes size.
    • Search's filter sheets offer what your ledger gains while they are open, and a payment method you chose then deleted can still be unticked.
    • Tapping a month in Spending by month keeps it under your finger wherever the chart stands.
    • Price check's currency list keeps its size when you turn an iPad.
    • Dialogs close at once if you turned on Reduce Motion while they were open.

  2. 0.46.0

    October 24, 2026

    New

    • Show map in Search opens the Spending map on your search's matches, over any dates and filters: each spot shows the net of its matches, money in shown in the income colour. Plus
    • A search's map lists its matches with Search's own selection: select on the map or in Search, select everything at a spot, and change the title, category or place right there. Plus
    • "Excludes …" under the Search net, and on a search's map, opens Not included, naming each currency All in can't convert yet and how many matches it leaves out.

  3. 0.45.0

    October 23, 2026

    New

    • Play on the Spending map replays the period day by day: each day's transactions land on the map in the order they happened, joining their spots as the total counts up, and the list fills in, newest day first. Plus
    • Choose how the map follows a replay, flying to each day or widening to take in everything so far, and switch between them while it plays. Plus
    • Stop, tapping a spot or a row, or changing the period ends a replay, leaving the map at rest as it was before. Plus

    Fixed

    • Changes made while a page was hidden, or under a drag, land at once instead of playing when you come back or trailing your finger.
    • Search starts on, and Reset returns it to, the same today as the rest of the app.

  4. 0.44.3

    October 22, 2026

    Fixed

    • With Reduce Motion on, forms, pages, dialogs, date and time pickers and the amount calculator appear and leave at once instead of sliding.
    • Editing a transaction that has no place, a place picked with Where I am now stays when you change the date.
    • Save on the Settings sheets matches the other wide buttons and fades on as you make a change.
    • The currencies to add and Send feedback's choices stand as tall as the other rows.
    • Backup & data and the light and dark choice in Settings sit evenly in their cards.

  5. 0.44.2

    October 22, 2026

    Fixed

    • Settings and every page it opens are laid out alike: the same spacing, rows and buttons throughout.
    • With Reduce Motion on in an iPhone's settings, Meeney's moves now land at once.
    • Home, Analytics, Add and Search turn to the new day at midnight, and when you come back to the app on a later day.
    • A new transaction you haven't touched yet moves its date and time to now when you come back to the app.
    • The recurring rules list and the date on a rule or transaction form stay right past midnight and into a new year.
    • Exchange rates a failed fetch missed are fetched again when you come back to the app.
    • Price check fetches the rate for its figures currency when it doesn't have it yet.
    • A no to Location on Welcome is kept, so adding a transaction doesn't ask again, and a phone that couldn't ask isn't taken as a no.
    • Backup & data's size leaves out places while Include places is off.

  6. 0.44.1

    October 21, 2026

    Fixed

    • With Ignore case off, Title Other's Show transactions, Spending map and Spending by time keep to Title Other's own titles, leaving out a top title written in another case. Plus
    • A place still being found when you save now lands on that transaction, even if you turn Demo mode on or off while it's found.
    • In a currency with no income, Where the money went lights the category you picked in the Breakdown. Plus
    • Editing a transaction keeps its place when the place loads while Add place's picker is open.
    • A place found late is no longer lost when you save an edit or change a title as it arrives.
    • A time range ending at 00:00 says it runs to 23:59, without crossing midnight.

    Faster

    • The Breakdown counts each slice's Show transactions only once, and only when it's shown.

  7. 0.44.0

    October 21, 2026

    New

    • See when your money moves with Spending by time, a new chart on Analytics: what a day spends by hour, by weekday or by day of month, each bar an average a day, with the period before faint behind it. Plus
    • Tap a bar to read it out, and Show opens Search on just that hour, weekday or date. Plus
    • On a tablet or a wide window, the hours spread across the week as a grid of dots. Plus
    • Group the Breakdown by part of day with the new Part of day lens: morning, afternoon, evening, night and no time. Plus
    • Choose Night and the Spending map goes dark. Plus
    • Search's new Weekday and Day of month filters find what you spend on Fridays, or what goes out on the 1st.

  8. 0.43.0

    October 21, 2026

    New

    • Transactions can keep the time of day they happened: anything dated today starts at the time now, and a chip beside the date sets or clears it.
    • Each row shows its time small under the amount, and a day lists the latest first.
    • Receipt fill also reads the time printed on the receipt, when it goes with the receipt's date.
    • Search's new Time filter finds what you bought in the morning, afternoon, evening or at night, in any range of hours, or with no time.
    • Turn off Time on new transactions in Settings › Adding transactions to leave new transactions without a time.
    • Demo mode's transactions have times too.

  9. 0.42.2

    October 20, 2026

    Fixed

    • A place still being found when you save no longer gets lost if changing that transaction's place by hand fails.
    • The spending map flies once to a new period's spots, rather than starting over as they arrive. Plus

  10. 0.42.1

    October 19, 2026

    Fixed

    • Pages scroll under a clear status bar: what's under it is no longer blurred or dimmed.

  11. 0.42.0

    October 18, 2026

    New

    • See where the money went on the Spending map, a new chart on Analytics framed around every place in the period. Plus
    • Tap the map to explore it: amounts merge as you zoom out and split as you zoom in, a tap on one flies in or picks it, and the sheet below lists the transactions in view. Plus
    • Group the Breakdown by country with the new Place lens; choosing a country narrows the Spending map and Breakdown by month, and Show opens Search filtered to it. Plus
    • The Breakdown's lenses are chips now, so all four fit on a phone.
    • A transaction with a place shows it on a small map, with Open in Maps for directions in your maps app.
    • Drop a pin on the map in the place picker to mark a spot search can't name, as precisely as you zoom in.
    • A place not named yet reads "Near Shibuya" from the map on your phone, rather than "Unnamed place".
    • Get map detail (towns, districts and roads) for any area under Settings › Map detail, or from the line at the foot of the map; it works offline once it's here.
    • The maps are drawn on your phone and send nothing; map detail you ask for comes from Meeney's public files on GitHub.

    Fixed

    • Add currency's suggestions close once the field holds a whole code, so they no longer cover Save on a phone.
    • On a wide window, the amount calculator and the place picker stop above the Shell bar rather than cutting through it.

  12. 0.41.0

    October 17, 2026

    New

    • Give a transaction its place with Add place: on a transaction dated today it reads where you are, and saving never waits for it; on any other day, pick one from your recent places, by name, or where you are now.
    • Name a place as you like ("Grandma's"), and go back to the name it was found as.
    • Every transaction row ends with its place, on Home, in Search and on Add's day page.
    • Abroad, one tap ("In Japan? Enter in JPY") puts every line of the form in the local currency.
    • Places saved offline get their names once your phone is back online.
    • Turn on Place on new transactions, under Settings › Adding transactions, to start every new transaction with where you are.
    • Search matches place names, filters by country or near a place, and Spent near here on a transaction shows what else you spent around it.
    • Set or remove a place on many transactions at once with Change › Place.
    • Backups carry your places unless you leave them out, and Remove all places forgets every place at once.
    • Demo mode has made-up places: a home in Tokyo and a trip to Bangkok.
    • Backup files carry your key height.

    Fixed

    • When a change can't be kept, Meeney now says so and puts things back as they were: settings and switches, reorders and moved tabs, a new category, currency or payment method, your own color sets, key height and list heights.
    • A form whose save or delete fails says so and stays open, a recurring rule whose first catch-up fails leaves nothing behind, and Welcome leaves nothing half-written when its save fails.
    • A failed Plus purchase says so and the buy button works again, and a Plus restore keeps what it found even when saving it fails.
    • Help, Privacy policy and Terms of use say so when they can't open, Copy log when it can't reach the clipboard, and a transaction when its receipts can't be read.
    • Settings sheets keep working when their action fails.
    • Home, Search and Price check start afresh after a restore.
    • Rule reminders are planned again once the phone lets them through after refusing. Plus

  13. 0.40.0

    October 16, 2026

    New

    • The debug log notes every failure the app meets, from a backup file that couldn't be read to a mail app that didn't open, a receipt photo that couldn't be read or a save that didn't go through, so what you paste shows why something went wrong.
    • Each event in the debug log is info, warning or error: warnings and errors are tinted and name their level, and Copy log adds the level, times to the millisecond, your phone's time zone and how many errors and warnings it holds.
    • The debug log keeps the last 400 info events and, apart from them, the last 100 warnings and errors, so a failure stays there after many ordinary events.
    • The debug log also notes demo mode turning on or off, Plus starting, ending or being restored, each catch-up of recurring rules, and currencies added, removed or made the default.

  14. 0.39.1

    October 15, 2026

    Fixed

    October 15, 2026

    Fixed

    • Income and expense by month stays in place when the window is resized as it glides. Plus
    • Leaving Home right after opening a day in another month no longer jumps back to that day.
    • Home's type filter stays on screen under an open month calendar at large text sizes.
    • Welcome's location suggestion no longer waits forever when the place lookup doesn't answer.
    • A transaction saved and then deleted no longer stays on Add's day page when Repeats is on.
    • Tapping an app icon as it goes back to Parchment's shows the Plus offer again.
    • On a tablet, turning to landscape no longer leaves the calculator over Add's day page, brings back a hidden tab bar, loses a copied color set's page in Settings, or replays motion on Home and Add when you come back to them.

  16. 0.38.0

    • On a tablet in landscape, Home shows the month beside its days, and tapping a day brings it to the top of the list.
    • On a tablet in landscape, Settings keeps its list beside the open page, and Search lays every filter open beside the matches.
    • On a tablet in landscape, Add shows the day your transaction goes to as you fill it in, and with Repeats on, the dates coming up.
    • On a tablet in landscape, Analytics sets its charts in two columns.

    Fixed

    Fixed
