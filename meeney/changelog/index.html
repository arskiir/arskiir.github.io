Changelog

What each version of Meeney brought, newest first.

  1. 0.33.0

    October 11, 2026

    New

    • Every payment method has its own emoji, shown before its name on the Via chips, in the Search filter and on the Settings row.
    • Pick a payment method's emoji in Settings › Payment methods; a new one starts on 💳, and Other keeps ❓.
    • Each payment method's emoji comes back when you restore a backup.
    • The payment method Breakdown labels each slice with its emoji. Plus

    Fixed

    • Other now comes last on the Via chips and in the Search filter, as Settings says.

  2. 0.32.1

    October 10, 2026

    Fixed

    • The bottom bar now glides back up smoothly when you scroll fast to the end of a page, instead of stalling and jumping into place.
