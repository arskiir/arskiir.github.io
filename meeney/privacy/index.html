Privacy policy

Took effect on October 10, 2026.

Who makes Meeney

Meeney is made by Sukrit Banthao, an individual developer. Write to meeneyapp@gmail.com with any question about this policy or your data.

Meeney collects nothing

Your ledger stays on your phone: every transaction, note, receipt and setting is kept there. There is no account to make, and no ads, analytics or tracking in the app. Nothing about you is sold or shared, because nothing about you reaches us.

What the app uses, and what leaves your phone

Day rates
To convert between currencies, Meeney asks Frankfurter for exchange rates. The request carries only currency codes and dates, never an amount or anything else from your ledger.
Location
Only when you ask Meeney to suggest the currency where you are. It asks for your approximate location, sends it to your phone's own geocoder to name the place and its country, and it is never kept.
Camera and photos
Used only when you add a receipt to a transaction. The photo is kept with that transaction, on your phone.
Plus
Paid for and checked through Apple or Google, in the App Store or Google Play. Meeney asks the store whether you have Plus, and sends nothing about it anywhere else.
Feedback
Sent from your own email app to meeneyapp@gmail.com, with what you write and the device info you see before sending. It is kept in that mailbox.
Your phone's own backup
iCloud Backup or Google's backup may copy Meeney's data to your own Apple or Google account, as it does for your other apps. Google stops backing up an app past 25 MB.

Keeping and deleting

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it with Reset all data, under Settings › Backup & data, or uninstall Meeney. A backup file you export stays wherever you put it.

To have your feedback deleted, write to meeneyapp@gmail.com and it will be.

Every request Meeney makes goes over HTTPS.
